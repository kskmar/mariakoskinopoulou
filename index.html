About me

I work on robotic perception and manipulation, with a particular interest in how robots can use vision, sensing and learning to interact with the physical world. My research spans medical robotics, precision assembly and robotic disassembly.

I am based at the National Robotarium, within the Institute of Signals, Sensors and Systems in the School of Engineering and Physical Sciences. I co-lead ARM²Lab with Dr Ignacio Carlucho.

I am Co-Director of the CDT in Dependable and Deployable AI for Robotics (D2AIR) and Deputy Postgraduate Research (PGR) Coordinator in the School of Engineering and Physical Sciences. I also coordinate Heriot-Watt’s PhD partnerships with Xidian University and Ocean University.

Before joining Heriot-Watt, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Biomedical Robotics Laboratory at the Italian Institute of Technology. I completed my PhD at the University of Crete, studying learning from demonstration for robotic manipulation.

Research interests

Vision-guided manipulation, multimodal sensing, learning from demonstration, and precision robotic intervention in healthcare and manufacturing.
