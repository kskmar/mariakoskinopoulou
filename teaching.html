Teaching & supervision

Learning by doing.

Connecting the foundations of vision and robotics with practical systems.

Machine Vision B31MV · MSc

My Machine Vision course introduces the principles behind visual perception and their use in real systems. Topics include image formation, image processing, machine learning for vision and 3D vision, with an emphasis on understanding how these components fit together.

Introduction to Robotics Undergraduate labs

I contribute to practical robotics teaching through hands-on laboratory sessions using Niryo Ned2 robots. Students explore robot operation and manipulation, including vision and conveyor-based tasks.

Student projects and supervision

I supervise MSc projects and PhD research in robotic perception, learning and manipulation. Project areas include medical robotics, precision optical assembly, adaptive disassembly and vision-based robot learning.

If you are interested in a project, email me with a brief description of your interests and background. Information about our PhD researchers is available on the ARM²Lab website.

Short courses

I have also delivered a compact robotics course in Xi’an, covering robotics fundamentals, ROS, Python, motion and manipulation, machine learning and autonomous robots.
