Research

Perception into action.

Enabling robots to sense, learn and manipulate in demanding environments.

Medical robotics

I investigate vision-guided robotic interventions in soft tissues, bringing together ultrasound, robotic control and multimodal sensing. I am interested in how complementary sensory information can support precise needle placement and more dependable interaction with deformable environments.

Precision manipulation and assembly

My work explores robotic handling and alignment of delicate components, including optical assembly. Combining the reach of a robot arm with fine local motion provides a route towards more dexterous manipulation and precise placement.

Robotic disassembly and learning

I study adaptive manipulation for the disassembly of electronic devices, including dual-arm coordination, visual recognition and task planning. A central interest is how robots can use prior experience and new observations to adapt to unfamiliar objects and changing tasks.

Earlier projects

Earlier work includes ANASA, on robotic sorting of recyclable materials; ENTIMENT, on temporal cognition for commercial robots; TIMESTORM, on the temporal aspects of human–machine interaction; and METR, on mental training in artificial agents.
